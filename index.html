The Hidden Order

A strategy board game of flowers and harmonies, played in virtual reality. Take your seat at a giant board in a quiet spirit world, grow your flowers, link them into harmonies, and be the first to close a ring around the centre.

PlatformMeta Quest 2, Pro, 3 and 3S
Players1 against the computer, or 2 taking turns
LanguagesEnglish, French
ControlsTouch controllers, seated
