Privacy Policy

The Hidden Order · last updated November 26, 2026

This policy explains what personal information The Hidden Order (“the game”) handles. The game is made by SHIBZ VR, an independent developer in Montreal, Quebec, Canada.

The short version

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information about you. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no online features.

What stays on your headset

The game saves your settings (for example text size, seat height, board position, computer speed and controller vibrations) on your headset only, so they are kept between sessions. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your headset’s settings, deletes them.

Services we don’t control

  • Meta. You get the game from the Meta Horizon Store and play it on a Meta Quest headset. Meta handles your account, purchases, downloads and headset data under its own policy: Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Unity. The game is built with the Unity engine. We have turned off Unity’s crash and diagnostic reports, so the game does not send them.

Children

The game is rated for teens and adults (13 and over) and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights and deleting data

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your settings can be removed at any time by uninstalling the game. If you have a question or a request about your privacy, contact the person responsible for personal information at SHIBZ VR: shibzvr@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling personal information (for example online leaderboards), we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
